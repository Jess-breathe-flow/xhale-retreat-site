Pay over time

Need longer? Choose a financing option like Affirm at checkout and spread it out over the months that work for you. Your room is paid in full, and you pay them back on their terms.

Cancel 90 or more days before the retreat and we refund everything beyond the deposit. Between 60 and 89 days, we refund half. Under 60 days, payments are non refundable. You can also transfer your place to someone else for a $100 fee, and we strongly recommend travel insurance.

Why you'll want travel insurance

Gili Air is a small island. There's a doctor on call, but anything serious means a trip to Bali or Lombok, and international flights and a non refundable deposit are real money to have riding on nothing. Travel insurance covers both ends of that: the cost of your trip if life gets in the way before you go, and medical care and evacuation if something happens while you're there.

Look for a policy that covers trip cancellation and interruption for the full non refundable cost of your retreat and flights, plus emergency medical care and medical evacuation for international travel. It's worth buying soon after you book, since most cancellation coverage only applies to things that come up after your policy starts.
