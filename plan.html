Step three

Getting to Gili Air

Fly into Bali (Denpasar, DPS), then take the fast boat to Gili Air. Here's what a round trip economy flight from the US typically runs. Fares change often, so treat these as a starting point.

West Coast$900 to $1,500Los Angeles and San Francisco are usually the least expensive way in.
Central US$1,100 to $1,700Chicago, Atlanta, Houston, Dallas, usually with one or two stops.
East Coast$1,100 to $1,800Boston, New York, Washington, usually with one or two stops.

Plan on about a day of travel each way, so about 10 days away in total. Book early for the best fares, since June is a busy time to fly to Bali.
