4. Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is required. Your policy should cover trip cancellation, medical care, emergency evacuation, and the activities offered on this retreat. Please carry a copy of your policy with you.

5. Health and Fitness

You are responsible for ensuring you are physically and mentally fit to participate. Please disclose any injuries, medical or mental health conditions, and dietary needs before you book. If any activity causes you pain or discomfort, stop immediately. If you are pregnant, please provide a letter from your healthcare provider confirming you are fit to travel and participate. In the interest of your wellbeing or that of the group, we reserve the right to ask you to rest from any activity.

You understand that travel, yoga, breathwork, water activities such as snorkeling and swimming, uneven island terrain, and other retreat activities carry inherent risks, including the risk of injury or illness. 8. Communicable Illness

You understand that group travel carries the risk of exposure to communicable illnesses. You agree not to attend if you are unwell or showing symptoms of a contagious illness at the start of the retreat. If you become unwell during the retreat, you agree to tell us and to take reasonable steps to protect yourself and others, including any local health requirements. Costs from separate accommodation, care, or travel due to illness are your responsibility, which is another reason travel insurance is required.

9. Cancellation by You

All cancellations must be made in writing. Your deposit is non-refundable at any time. If you cancel more than 90 days before the retreat, you will receive a refund of payments made beyond your deposit. If you cancel between 90 and 60 days before, 50 percent of the balance beyond your deposit may be refunded. Within 60 days of the retreat, no refund is available. If another guest can take your place, we may offer a refund at our discretion. There are no refunds for arriving late, leaving early, or any unused portion of the retreat.

11. Cancellation by Us

We reserve the right to cancel the retreat. This retreat requires a minimum number of confirmed guests. If that minimum is not reached by 60 days before the start date, or if events beyond our control require it, we may cancel and refund your payments to us, though we are not responsible for your travel costs. 12. Personal Property

You will have a private room and your own key. Keeping your belongings safe is your responsibility. We are not liable for lost, stolen, or damaged personal property.

13. Community Agreement

Xhale is a shared and sacred space. You agree to treat fellow guests, facilitators, and staff, and the island and its community, with respect and care. We reserve the right to ask anyone whose behavior harms the group or the space to leave, without refund.
