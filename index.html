The week

Every day starts with a gentle practice, holds one or two moments together, and leaves plenty of space for you. Tap a day to picture yourself there.

Day 1 · Arrive

Threshold

The day you finally put it down.

There are no cars on Gili Air. Tonight the loudest thing you'll hear is the water.

  1. Midday

    The boat from Bali carries you across the water. Somewhere between the harbor and the island, your shoulders start to drop.

  2. Afternoon

    Bare feet on the sand. A cool drink, a welcome gift, and your bungalow. Unpack, or don't. Nap, swim, wander.

    Free time
  3. Welcome gift

    Your own Xhale journal, waiting in your room. You'll use it all week, and take it home.

  4. Sunset

    Our opening circle with Jess and Aleisha as the light goes gold. A gentle ritual to set down what you carried here, so you don't have to hold it all week.

    Included
  5. Evening

    Our first dinner together. No introductions you have to perform. Just faces you'll know by the end of the week.

    Included

Questions

What people ask

Who is Xhale for?

Anyone who carries a lot and is ready to set it down. Wherever you come from and whatever you're carrying, you're welcome here, exactly as you are.

Do I need yoga or breathwork experience?

No. Every practice works for every body and every level, and every session is optional.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Most people do. The group is small, and you'll know everyone by the second day.

How active is the week?

As active as you want it to be. Snorkeling and biking are there if you want them, and so are naps.

How much time off will I need?

Plan on about 10 days. The retreat is seven nights, and the flight from the US to Bali takes about a day each way. Many people add a night in Bali to rest before the boat.

Can I bring a friend or partner?

Yes. Xhale is open to people of all genders, and coming with someone you love is welcome. Shared bungalows are a good fit for two, at $2,400 per person.

Is it safe? What is the island like?

Gili Air is a small, friendly, walkable island with no cars, popular with travelers who want a quiet stay. We have a trusted contact on the island, and the bungalows, gardens, and spa are reserved just for our group.

What about food needs, accessibility, or health?

Tell us what you need when you reserve, and we'll plan around it. The island has sandy paths and no cars, so getting around means walking, biking, or a horse cart. If that's a concern, ask us and we'll talk it through.

Will I have wifi?

Yes, if you need it. We'll gently invite you to unplug, but nothing is required.

How do I get there?

Fly into Bali (Denpasar). Your airport and harbor transfers and the fast boat to Gili Air are included. Your passport should be valid for at least six months after travel. We'll send a full travel guide when you reserve.

What if I need to cancel?

Your $500 deposit is non refundable. If you cancel 90 or more days before the retreat, we refund everything beyond the deposit. Between 60 and 89 days, we refund half. Under 60 days, payments are non refundable. You can also transfer your place to someone else. We strongly recommend travel insurance.
