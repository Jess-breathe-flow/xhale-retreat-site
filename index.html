This is for you

You're the one who shows up every day, no matter what. The leader everyone leans on. The parent who never clocks out. The friend who always picks up the late night call. The one who holds the team together, then goes home and holds everyone else together too. You've learned to be twice as prepared, to shift how you show up depending on the room, and to hold it together in spaces that weren't built for you.

You're so used to carrying it all that you don't even notice the weight anymore. You just know you're tired in a way sleep doesn't fix.

When was the last time you took a real breath? The deep kind that reaches all the way down and lets your shoulders drop.

The whole of it

All of life, in one week

Xhale isn't a retreat where you sit still for seven days. It's rest, and it's also adventure. You'll breathe and stretch by the water, and you'll snorkel with sea turtles. You'll lie under the stars for a sound bath, and you'll dance barefoot in the sand. You'll taste the island's food, ride its sandy paths, and spend time in a culture that moves at a different pace than home.

Every piece was chosen on purpose. Stillness and adventure, quiet and laughter, the familiar and the new. Together they wake up your senses and remind you what life is supposed to feel like. And somewhere between the sound bath and the dance party, things start to shift. You loosen your grip on where you've been, step a little outside your comfort zone, and start asking a better question: what do I want next?

  • BreatheBreathwork, yoga, and sound to settle your body.
  • RestThe pool, the spa, and long, quiet afternoons.
  • ExploreTurtles, sandy bike paths, and a day on Gili T.
  • PlayA beach dance party and laughter you forgot you had.
  • ConnectCircles, shared dinners, and a group that becomes yours.
  • ReflectGuided sessions and your journal, to ask what's next.

Inside the guided sessions

Simple tools you'll actually use at home

Jess and Aleisha lead four small, gentle sessions during the week. Each one blends a little guidance, quiet time with your Xhale journal, and conversation in pairs or as a circle. There's nothing to prepare and nothing to perform. You share only what you want, and you're always welcome to just listen.

1Day 2 · 60 min · Jess

Remember who you are

Under all the roles you play, there's still you. In this gentle circle, Jess guides you back to who you are underneath everything you do for everyone else.

2Day 2 · 30 min · Aleisha

Cultivating gratitude

Gratitude is one of the simplest ways to change how a day feels. Aleisha shares why it works and a few easy ways to practice it, like journaling. Then you'll take a few quiet minutes to write, and start a practice you can come back to all week.

Most of us know we're tired, but few of us know what actually fills us back up. You'll look at why recharging matters, what you usually do to "recharge," and whether it really works. Then you'll map what truly restores you, and how to fit it into an ordinary week.

Where you'll stay

A resort that's ours for the week

Picture your week here

  1. Step out of your bungalow, and you're at the pool. All 17 bungalows sit in a ring around one big lagoon pool, so the water is right outside your door.
  2. The spa is in the middle of the circle. Your massage is only a few steps from your terrace.
  3. The beach is about a minute away. Cross the little lane to South Beach and the Lagoon Beach Club, with its infinity pool and sea views. It stays open to the public, so you may see a few other faces there.
  4. The harbor is about a five minute walk. The fast boat lands just down the path, so arriving is easy.
  5. The rest of the island is yours. Cafés and little shops are a few minutes away, and you can walk all the way around Gili Air in about an hour.

Questions

What people ask

Who is Xhale for?

Anyone who carries a lot and is ready to set it down. Wherever you come from and whatever you're carrying, you're welcome here, exactly as you are.

Do I need yoga or breathwork experience?

No. Every practice works for every body and every level, and every session is optional.

What are the guided sessions, and how involved do I need to be?

Jess and Aleisha lead four short, gentle sessions during the week: Remember who you are with Jess, and Cultivating gratitude (30 min), Lighten your life (60 min), and Your recharge map (60 min) with Aleisha. Each one blends a little teaching, quiet journaling, and conversation. You don't need to prepare or bring anything, since your journal and materials are waiting for you. Like everything at Xhale, they're an invitation, not a requirement. Join in fully, sit back and listen, or skip one for a nap. Sharing is always your choice.

Can I come on my own, or bring a friend or partner?

Either way. Most people come on their own, the group is small and you'll know everyone by the second day. Xhale is open to people of all genders, so coming with someone you love is welcome too. Shared bungalows are a good fit for two, at $2,400 per person.

How active is the week?

As active as you want it to be. Snorkeling and biking are there if you want them, and so are naps.

How much time off will I need?

Plan on about 10 days. The retreat is seven nights, and the flight from the US to Bali takes about a day each way. Many people add a night in Bali to rest before the boat.

Is it safe? What is the island like?

Gili Air is a small, friendly island with no cars and a slower, quieter pace than its party neighbor, Gili T. Crime is low, and what travelers do run into is mostly small stuff like petty theft. We still plan for your safety, and we hope you'll keep these in mind:

  • We believe in the buddy system. Heading out to explore, swim, or walk back after dark? Go with a friend from the group, and let one of us know where you're off to.
  • Do what feels safe for you. Every outing is optional. If you'd rather stay in, you can eat at the resort restaurant any time instead of heading out.
  • Bring a little light. The paths are sandy and some aren't lit at night, so a small flashlight or your phone light helps.
  • Swim with the group. There are no lifeguards on the beaches, so we snorkel together with local guides.
  • Keep it simple. Drink bottled water, stick to drinks from real bars and restaurants, and keep valuables in your in room safe.

And you're never on your own: there's a doctor on call around the clock, a front desk open 24 hours, a trusted contact of ours on the island, and the two of us with you all week.

What about food needs, accessibility, or health?

Tell us what you need when you reserve, and we'll plan around it. The island has sandy paths and no cars, so getting around means walking, biking, or a horse cart. If that's a concern, ask us and we'll talk it through.

Will I have wifi?

Yes. There's free wifi in your room and around the resort. We'll gently invite you to unplug, but nothing is required.

How do I get there?

Fly into Bali (Denpasar). Your airport and harbor transfers and the fast boat to Gili Air are included. Your passport should be valid for at least six months after travel. We'll send a full travel guide when you reserve.

What if I need to cancel?

Your $500 deposit is non refundable. If you cancel 90 or more days before the retreat, we refund everything beyond the deposit. Between 60 and 89 days, we refund half. Under 60 days, payments are non refundable. You can also transfer your place to someone else for a $100 fee. We strongly recommend travel insurance.
